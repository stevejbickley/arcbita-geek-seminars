### Session Title
> Document Extraction Techniques

### Presenter(s)
> Tim Nguyen, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> This seminar explores how document extraction techniques can be combined with AI agents to transform unstructured documents into structured, predefined outputs. The focus is on the overall workflow of extracting useful information from complex documents, preparing that information for AI processing, and guiding agents to return consistent results based on a defined schema.
>
> The presentation will discuss practical considerations when working with different document elements, including text, paragraphs, tables, figures, images, and other structured or semi-structured content. It will also examine challenges such as preserving context, managing large documents, reducing information loss, controlling token usage, and maintaining output consistency.
>
> Rather than presenting a single best approach, the seminar will introduce several possible processing strategies and their trade-offs. It is intended as an open technical discussion, welcoming alternative methods and ideas for improving document extraction and AI-agent workflows.
