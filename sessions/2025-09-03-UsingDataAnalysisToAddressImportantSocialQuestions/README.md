### Session Title
> “Using data analysis to address important social questions”

### Presenter(s)
> Dr Tony Beatton, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> This Geek Seminar explores how data analysis can be a powerful tool for understanding and tackling some of the most pressing social challenges we face today. From health and education to transport, sustainability, and community wellbeing, data helps us uncover patterns, test assumptions, and generate insights that can shape smarter decisions and policies.
>
> This session, facilitated by Dr Tony Beatton, will introduce practical approaches to working with data, showing how even simple techniques can reveal meaningful trends and inform better solutions. We’ll also discuss how behavioural and contextual factors can be integrated into data analysis to capture the complexity of real-world problems.

### Learning outcomes
- Recognise when and why to use panel (longitudinal) data instead of cross-sections; explain fixed effects, temporal ordering, and typical pitfalls (attrition, measurement error, multi-level structure).
- Diagnose and treat missing data: profile patterns, decide between complete-case, single imputation, interpolation, and (when appropriate) multiple imputation — knowing trade-offs and biases.
- Navigate ethics, governance, and access: data-management plans, information security, storage/HPC needs, and approvals for HILDA/ABS/agency data.
- Use PanelWhiz to retrieve and self-document panel data (e.g. HILDA) extracts efficiently, including plugins and required citation/donation practices.

### Who should attend
- Researchers, policy analysts, and social scientists working with survey or administrative data (education, health, crime, labour, wellbeing).
- Data engineers/research software analysts supporting empirical projects (Stata-based workflows, storage/HPC, governance).
- HDR students starting empirical chapters using HILDA/ABS/agency datasets.
