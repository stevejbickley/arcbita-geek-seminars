### Session Title
> A Practical Guide to Synthetic Data Generation

### Presenter(s)
> Arian Marshhady, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> In this session, Arian will cover what synthetic data is and why we'd bother using it in the first place. He’ll then move into the practical workflow: preparing data for synthesis, generating synthetic datasets, and evaluating both quality and privacy protection.
>
> Arian will compare different models such as Gaussian Copula, CTGAN, TVAE, and CopulaGAN, exploring their differences, when to use each one, and how they stack up against each other. He’ll dive into the mathematics behind some of these models to give a better understanding of what's actually happening under the hood. Then, he’ll explore conditional versus unconditional sampling approaches, what constraints are and why they matter for maintaining logical data relationships, and how to actually implement them in practice. 
> 
> Finally, Arian will discuss how to evaluate data quality and privacy through different tests. How to interpret results and make sense of them.

### Learning outcomes
- Understand what synthetic data is, its key use cases, and why it’s valuable for research and applied settings.
- Prepare, generate, and evaluate synthetic datasets using different models and workflows.
- Distinguish between Gaussian Copula, CTGAN, TVAE, and CopulaGAN models and know when to apply each.
- Apply conditional and constrained sampling techniques to maintain data realism and logical consistency.
- Assess data quality and privacy protection using appropriate evaluation metrics and interpret the results.

### Who should attend
- Researchers, data scientists, data engineers, policy analysts, and others working with sensitive or limited datasets who want to safely simulate or augment data for analysis and model development.
