### Session Title
> Agentic Tasks and Workflows

### Presenter(s)
> Arian Marshhady, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> This session introduces agentic systems by examining their definitions, core concepts, and significance. It also explores their practical use cases before moving on to analyse the key components that make up these systems.
>
> **Key Topics**
> 
> * **Tool Calling:** We introduce the concept of tool calling and provide relevant examples. This includes an examination of tokenizers, search tools, and rerankers, with a particular focus on cross-encoders. We also explore retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) in detail, covering its theoretical foundations and presenting a range of examples. 
> * **Open-weight and Closed LLMs:** We examine how both open-weight and closed-source models fit within agentic systems and consider their respective roles. 
> * **Workflow and Orchestration:** We provide an introduction to workflow design and orchestration, supported by specific workflow examples. 
>
> The session explains how these different components work together to form a complete system. It concludes with a comprehensive example that brings these elements together into a single integrated workflow.
