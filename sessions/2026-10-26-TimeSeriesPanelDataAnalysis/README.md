### Session Title
> Time Series / Panel Data Analysis

### Presenter(s)
> Dr Tony Beatton, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> How can we make sense of data that evolves over time and across individuals, organisations, or regions? In this seminar, Tony takes a hands-on approach to time series and panel data analysis, exploring how these methods can help uncover trends, identify relationships, and account for unobserved heterogeneity.
> 
> Rather than relying on static snapshots, the session focuses on exploiting the temporal and cross-sectional structure of data to generate richer insights and more robust empirical findings. The emphasis is on practical implementation, intuition, and real-world applications in terms of showing how these techniques can strengthen research design and support evidence-based decision-making.
