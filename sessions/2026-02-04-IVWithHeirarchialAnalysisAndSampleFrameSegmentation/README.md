### Session Title
> Use of IV with hierarchical analysis and sample frame segmentation to examine crime reducing policy alternatives to reduce crime among adolescents

### Presenter(s)
> Dr Tony Beatton, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> How can we design crime-reducing policies that actually work for adolescents? In this seminar, Dr Tony Beatton takes a hands-on approach to using instrumental variables (IV) combined with hierarchical analysis and sample-frame segmentation to evaluate policy alternatives. Rather than treating youth as a homogeneous group, the focus is on exploiting heterogeneity across sample frames to identify where and for whom policies are most effective. The session emphasises practical implementation, intuition, and policy relevance - showing how careful empirical design can generate actionable evidence for optimising crime-reduction strategies.
