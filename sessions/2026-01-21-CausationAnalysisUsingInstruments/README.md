### Session Title
> Foundation Statistics and Econometrics – Causation Analysis using Instruments

### Presenter(s)
> Dr Tony Beatton, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> In this session, Tony will walk through the practical application of IV methods in STATA, with a clear comparison of IVREGRESS versus IVREG2. The seminar will highlight when and why to use each approach, key differences in estimation and
 diagnostics, and common pitfalls to watch out for.

### Who should attend
- Ideal for researchers and analysts interested in strengthening causal claims in empirical work.
