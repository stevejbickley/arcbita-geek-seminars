### Session Title
> Are AI Methods Valid?

### Presenter(s)
> Dr Tony Beatton, ARC Centre for Behavioural Insights for Technology Adoption (ARC BITA) at QUT

### Abstract
> In his presentation, Tony will discuss Advancements in Artificial Intelligence Models (AI) and the development of Application Programming Interfaces (API) to make them easier to use offers new method opportunities for our research.
>
> This geek seminar seeks to initiate a debate on whether AI-based methods are scientifically valid and acceptable methods to use in social science research.
