### Session Title
> Research Ethics and Integrity

### Presenter(s)
> Professor Lynn Woodward, Chair of the University Human Research Ethics Committee (UHREC)

### Abstract
> Professor Woodward will begin with a brief background on research ethics before delving into the specific processes and practices at QUT that support responsible and ethical research. The session will highlight how QUT’s approach aligns with the Australian Code for the Responsible Conduct of Research, built on the following key principles:
>
> * Honesty in the development, undertaking and reporting of research 
> * Rigour in the design, conduct and communication of research 
> * Transparency in declaring interests and reporting research methodology, data and findings 
> * Fairness in the treatment of others 
> * Respect for research participants, the wider community, animals and the environment 
> * Recognition of the right of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to be engaged in research that affects or is of particular significance to them 
> * Accountability for the development, undertaking, and reporting of research 
> * Promotion of responsible research practices
>
> This seminar will provide valuable insights into QUT’s policies, guidance, and training in research integrity, as well as practical advice for researchers and supervisors.

### Learning outcomes
- Understand how QUT’s research integrity framework aligns with the Australian Code for the Responsible Conduct of Research. 
- Recognise and apply key principles of honest, rigorous, transparent and accountable research practice. 
- Identify common ethical issues in research and know how to address them using QUT’s policies, processes and support. 
- Understand obligations around fairness, respect, and engagement with Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples in research. 
- Know where to access QUT’s guidance, training and resources on responsible and ethical research conduct.

### Who should attend
- QUT HDR students, early and mid-career researchers, supervisors, and professional staff involved in research design, review, management or governance.
